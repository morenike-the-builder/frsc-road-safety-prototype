MODULE 1
Introduction to Road Safety

What is Road Safety?

Road safety is about protecting yourself and others on the road through proper driving practices and hazard awareness.

Why It Matters in Nigeria

  • Speeding - 40% of accidents
  • Reckless driving - Unsafe lane changes
  • Driver fatigue - Slower reaction times
  • Poor maintenance - Brake failure
  • Hazard unawareness - Not seeing dangers

Your Responsibility

  • Obey traffic laws and signals
  • Maintain your vehicle properly
  • Drive only when alert and sober
  • Respect other road users
  • Anticipate and avoid hazards

The Three Types of Traffic Signs

1. Regulatory Signs (Red/White)

Tell you what you MUST do or MUST NOT do - violating them is illegal.

2. Warning Signs (Yellow Diamond)

Alert you to potential hazards ahead. Slow down and be prepared.

3. Informational Signs (Blue/Green)

Provide helpful information about services and directions.

Road Markings on Pavement

  • White Broken Line - You CAN cross
  • White Solid Line - You CANNOT cross
  • Yellow Solid - No passing zone

MODULE 3
Road Rules & Regulations

Speed Limits in Nigeria

  • Urban areas: 50 km/h
  • Highways: 100 km/h
  • School zones: 40 km/h

Lane Discipline

  • Keep right except when overtaking
  • Signal before changing lanes
  • The 2-second rule: Keep 2 seconds behind car ahead

Mandatory Requirements

  • Wear seat belts
  • Valid license required
  • Third-party insurance mandatory
MODULE 4
Defensive Driving

The SIPDE Method

  • Scan - Look 200+ meters ahead
  • Identify - Spot hazards
  • Predict - Anticipate what might happen
  • Decide - Choose your action
  • Execute - Perform smoothly

The 2-Second Rule

Ensures safe following distance. Pick an object ahead, count when car passes it - you should reach it after counting 2 seconds.

MODULE 5
Vehicle Familiarisation

Essential Vehicle Systems

  • Braking System - Check regularly for responsiveness
  • Steering - Should be responsive and smooth
  • Lights - All must be functional
  • Tires - Check pressure and tread depth
  • Wipers - Essential for visibility

Pre-Drive Checks

  • Check mirrors and adjust seat
  • Verify all lights work
  • Test brakes before driving
  • Check tire condition
MODULE 6
Emergency Response & First Aid

Emergency Response

  • Brake failure: Use handbrake, downshift
  • Skidding: Don't brake, ease off accelerator
  • Tire blowout: Hold steering steady, ease off accelerator

First Aid Basics

  • Call emergency services (112/199)
  • Check for injuries and bleeding
  • Don't move accident victim unless in danger
  • Keep accident scene safe
MODULE 7
Navigation & Responsible Mobility

Route Planning

  • Plan your route before driving
  • Know current traffic conditions
  • Identify rest stops on long drives
  • Share your route with someone

Responsible Mobility

  • Don't drive if tired - take breaks every 2 hours
  • Never drive under influence of alcohol/drugs
  • No texting or phone use while driving
  • Respect speed limits and other road users
