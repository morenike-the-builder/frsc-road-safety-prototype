# FRSC Driver Licensing Guide - Complete Reference

## National Student Road Safety & Responsible Mobility Framework

This guide is based on the **FRSC Decentralised Standardization Support Programme (DSSP)**, which establishes standards for safe and responsible mobility among students in Nigeria.

---

## 🏛️ About FRSC (Federal Road Safety Corps)

### Statutory Role
The Federal Road Safety Corps is the primary regulatory body responsible for:
- Establishing national traffic and safety standards
- Licensing drivers and issuing driver's licenses
- Enforcing traffic laws and regulations
- Preventing road crashes and injuries
- Managing emergency response services
- Coordinating road safety education

### FRSC Authority in Driver Licensing
- **Only authority** that can issue official Nigerian driver's licenses
- Maintains national driver database
- Determines licensing standards and requirements
- Conducts licensing examinations
- Issues, renews, and revokes licenses
- Manages learner's permits

### Contact FRSC
- **Emergency Line:** 122 (toll-free, nationwide)
- **Website:** www.frsc.gov.ng
- **Local Offices:** In every state and major city

## 📋 Types of Licenses Available

### Private Drive (PVT) License
- **Vehicle Type:** Personal cars, vans, light commercial vehicles
- **Weight Limit:** Up to 3,500 kg
- **Passengers:** Maximum specified by vehicle class
- **Course:** Standard 15-module program (this course)
- **Duration:** 2 years minimum learner permit → 5-year license
- **Renewal:** Every 5 years

### Heavy Truck (HT) License
- **Vehicle Type:** Articulated trucks, heavy commercial vehicles
- **Weight:** Over 3,500 kg
- **Specialization:** Requires additional heavy vehicle training
- **Duration:** 3-5 year license
- **Medical:** More stringent medical requirements

### Public Transportation (PT) License
- **Vehicle Type:** Buses, minibuses, commercial taxis
- **Passengers:** Designed for transporting multiple passengers
- **Insurance:** Mandatory public liability insurance
- **Medical:** Annual medical certifications
- **Duration:** 2-3 year license

### Motorcycle (MCL) License
- **Vehicle Type:** Motorcycles, scooters, tricycles
- **Engine Size:** Up to specific displacement limits
- **Specialization:** Dedicated motorcycle safety training
- **Duration:** 2-5 year license
- **Helmet Requirements:** Mandatory

## 🎓 Learner's Permit Requirements

### Purpose of Learner's Permit
Learner's permits allow supervised driving practice before obtaining a full license. They bridge theoretical knowledge and practical experience.

### Learner's Permit Qualifications
To obtain a learner's permit, you must:
- ✓ Be at least 18 years of age
- ✓ Have completed secondary education (or equivalent)
- ✓ Be physically and medically fit to drive
- ✓ Pass medical examination
- ✓ Provide proof of identity
- ✓ Complete initial road safety awareness session
- ✓ Pass basic theory test (road signs, speed limits, laws)

### Learner's Permit Validity
- **Initial Issue:** Valid for 12 months
- **Renewal:** Can be renewed for additional 12 months
- **Maximum Duration:** Typically 24-36 months maximum
- **Conversion:** Can be converted to full license after practicum

### Learner's Permit Restrictions
Under a learner's permit, you **MUST:**
- ✓ Display "L" plate on front and rear
- ✓ Drive only on specified routes/areas
- ✓ Have licensed driver (18+) in front seat
- ✓ Not carry passengers except supervising driver
- ✓ Not drive between 10 PM - 6 AM (some states)
- ✓ Respect all traffic laws (same as full license holders)
- ✓ Carry learner's permit at all times

## 🏥 Medical Fitness Examination

### FRSC Medical Centers
FRSC partners with authorized medical centers where you must have:

### Medical Tests Required
- **Visual Acuity Test:** Must see 6/9 or better (with correction if needed)
- **Color Vision Test:** Ability to distinguish traffic lights
- **Eye Pressure:** Check for glaucoma
- **Hearing Test:** Ability to hear normal conversation and signals
- **Blood Pressure:** Must be within safe driving range
- **Blood Sugar Test:** To rule out diabetes complications
- **General Health:** Overall fitness for driving

### Medical Conditions Requiring Disclosure
Must disclose any of:
- Epilepsy or seizure disorders
- Diabetes (if on insulin)
- Heart conditions or high blood pressure
- Vision problems even if corrected
- Hearing problems even if using aids
- Psychological conditions affecting judgment
- Substance abuse history
- Previous serious driving violations

### Medical Certificate Validity
- **New License:** Valid for 5 years
- **Age 55+:** Valid for 3 years (more frequent checks)
- **Age 70+:** Annual medical re-certification required

### Cost of Medical Examination
- Typically ₦2,000-₦5,000 at FRSC centers
- Some centers charge per test performed
- Receipt must be presented with application

## 🗓️ The Licensing Appointment Process

### How to Schedule an Appointment

**Option 1: Online Booking**
1. Visit FRSC website (www.frsc.gov.ng)
2. Navigate to "Online Licensing Services"
3. Select your state and preferred center
4. Choose available appointment date/time
5. Provide personal information
6. Confirm booking and receive reference number
7. Print or screenshot confirmation

**Option 2: Direct Office Visit**
1. Visit your nearest FRSC licensing office
2. Request appointment with staff
3. Provide preferred dates/times
4. Get appointment card with date and time
5. Be advised of required documents

**Option 3: Through Cubbes Platform**
1. In the Booking section, select "Schedule FRSC Appointment"
2. Choose your state and preferred office
3. Select available appointment date
4. Submit through Cubbes
5. Receive reference and reminder notifications

### Appointment Confirmation
You'll receive:
- [ ] Appointment reference number
- [ ] Specific date and time
- [ ] FRSC office location and address
- [ ] Parking and entry instructions
- [ ] List of required documents
- [ ] Cost breakdown for all fees
- [ ] Preparation guidelines

### Before Your Appointment
- [ ] Gather all required documents
- [ ] Make copies (keep originals)
- [ ] Complete medical examination
- [ ] Get recent passport photos
- [ ] Check appointment details carefully
- [ ] Plan arrival time (arrive 30 minutes early)
- [ ] Arrange transportation

### On Your Appointment Day
- **Arrive Early:** 30 minutes before scheduled time
- **Dress Professionally:** Neat, clean, presentable
- **Bring Everything:** All required documents in order
- **Be Prepared:** To answer questions about your training
- **Bring Valid ID:** Matching your application documents
- **Have Payment:** Cash or card for all fees
- **Be Patient:** Process can take 2-4 hours

## 📸 What Happens During Your Licensing Visit

### Step 1: Document Verification (30-45 minutes)
- Staff verify all submitted documents
- Check completeness of application
- Cross-reference with training records
- Flag any missing documents
- Request additional info if needed

### Step 2: Biometric Capture (15-20 minutes)
- Fingerprint scanning (10 digits)
- Facial photograph with specifications
- Digital signature capture
- Iris recognition (some centers)
- Verification of captured data

### Step 3: Identity Interview (20-30 minutes)
- FRSC official reviews your application
- Confirms personal information accuracy
- Discusses your training background
- Questions about Cubbes course and learning
- Verification of practical driving experience
- Discussion of driving responsibilities

### Step 4: Written/Digital Theory Exam (30-45 minutes)
- Multiple-choice questions on Nigerian traffic laws
- Road sign identification (20+ signs)
- Hazard perception scenarios
- Safe driving principles
- Emergency procedures
- Penalty and fine questions
- Minimum pass score: 60-70% (varies by state)

**Typical Exam Questions:**
- "What is the speed limit in built-up areas in Nigeria?"
- "What does a circular red sign with white border mean?"
- "How far ahead should you scan the road?"
- "What is the 3-second rule?"
- "What should you do if you have a tire blowout?"
- "When is a license suspended in Nigeria?"

### Step 5: Payment Processing (10-15 minutes)
- Confirm all fees (application, license, processing)
- Accept payment (cash, card, transfer)
- Issue official receipt
- Provide payment reference

### Step 6: License Issuance (varies)
- **Same Day:** Some offices issue same day (rare)
- **1-2 Weeks:** Most common turnaround
- **2-4 Weeks:** If additional processing needed
- **Email Notification:** You'll be notified when ready for pickup
- **License Presentation:** Official presentation ceremony or collection

## 📜 License Validity & Renewal

### License Validity Period
- **Initial License:** 5 years from issue date
- **Adult Renewal:** 5 years each renewal
- **Senior (55-70 years):** 3 years validity
- **Senior (70+ years):** 1-2 years + annual medical

### Renewal Process
Renewal happens **before expiration:**
1. Apply 6 months before expiration
2. Submit renewal application form
3. Medical examination (if required)
4. Pay renewal fees (typically ₦5,000-₦10,000)
5. Receive updated license

### License Suspension or Revocation
Your license can be suspended if you:
- ❌ Accumulate traffic violations/points
- ❌ Drive under influence of alcohol/drugs
- ❌ Cause accidents with injuries
- ❌ Drive with cancelled/revoked license
- ❌ Fail to pay fines or penalties
- ❌ Medical fitness concerns
- ❌ Provide false information during licensing

### Replacement of Lost/Damaged License
If your license is lost, stolen, or damaged:
1. Report to FRSC immediately
2. File police report (for theft/loss)
3. Submit replacement application
4. Pay replacement fee (typically ₦3,000-₦5,000)
5. Provide recent photograph
6. Receive new license in 5-7 days

## ⚖️ Traffic Laws & Your Responsibilities

### Speed Limits in Nigeria
- **Built-up Areas:** Maximum 50 km/h
- **Urban Roads:** Maximum 80 km/h
- **Expressways:** Maximum 100 km/h
- **Highways:** Maximum 80-100 km/h (sign-dependent)

### Right-of-Way Rules
- ✓ Pedestrians have right-of-way at crossings
- ✓ Emergency vehicles have absolute right-of-way
- ✓ From right side at equal intersections
- ✓ To vehicles already in roundabout
- ✓ Oncoming traffic at yellow lights

### Safe Driving Requirements
- ✓ Maintain valid license and documents
- ✓ Wear seatbelt (all occupants)
- ✓ Don't use mobile phone while driving
- ✓ Don't drive under alcohol influence (0% limit)
- ✓ Don't drive while fatigued
- ✓ Maintain vehicle in safe condition
- ✓ Display valid license plates
- ✓ Carry valid insurance certificate

### Penalties & Fines

| Offense | Penalty | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Speeding | ₦10,000-₦50,000 | Warning or fine |
| No license plate | ₦25,000 | Impound vehicle |
| Reckless driving | ₦50,000+ | Suspension possible |
| Driving under influence | ₦100,000+ | License suspension |
| Hit and run | ₦200,000+ | Criminal charges |
| Traffic violation points | 12 points | License revocation |

## 🎯 Safety Reminders for New Drivers

### First 6 Months
- **Defensive Focus:** Prioritize defense over offense
- **Extra Caution:** Be extra careful in new situations
- **No Passengers:** Avoid distracting passengers initially
- **Daylight Driving:** Restrict night driving if possible
- **Familiar Routes:** Master known routes before exploring
- **Weather Awareness:** Extra caution in rain/weather
- **Fatigue:** Rest immediately if feeling tired

### Ongoing Responsibilities
- **Regular Maintenance:** Service vehicle per schedule
- **License Renewal:** Don't let license expire
- **Medical:** Maintain health to keep driving safe
- **Training:** Consider advanced driving courses
- **Attitude:** Never be aggressive or overconfident
- **Others:** Respect all road users equally
- **Emergency:** Know how to call FRSC (122)

### Safe Driving Mindset
> *"A good driver is one who goes home the same way they left home - safely."*

- View driving as a responsibility, not a right
- Every vehicle has someone's loved ones inside
- Road safety is everyone's responsibility
- Your license is a privilege to be respected
- One mistake can change lives forever
