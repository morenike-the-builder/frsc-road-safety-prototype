## ⚡ PHASE 2: Simulation & Driving Readiness

**Status:** Unlocks after Phase 1 completion  
**Duration:** 1 session (2 hours)  
**Format:** Hardware driving simulator  
**Location:** FRSC-Certified simulation hubs  
**Cost:** ₦5,000 per session

### Why Simulation?

Simulation provides an important bridge between theoretical learning and real-world driving. It allows learners to:
- Build practical judgment in a safe environment
- Make mistakes without risk
- Develop hazard perception skills
- Experience emergency scenarios
- Increase driving confidence

### Simulator Activities

#### 1. **Vehicle Familiarisation**
- Learn all controls and dashboard indicators
- Understand mirrors and blind spots
- Practice seat, mirror, and pedal adjustment
- Get comfortable with vehicle responsiveness

#### 2. **Steering & Vehicle Control**
- Smooth acceleration and braking
- Proper steering wheel technique
- Understanding vehicle weight and momentum
- Response to different surfaces
- Gear selection (where applicable)

#### 3. **Hazard Perception**
- Identify potential road hazards
- Reactive decision-making exercises
- Speed appropriate responses
- Anticipatory scanning
- Environmental awareness

#### 4. **Junction Navigation**
- Approaching intersections safely
- Traffic light interpretation
- Roundabout navigation
- Merging and yielding
- Complex multi-lane junctions

#### 5. **Parking & Lane Discipline**
- Parallel parking technique
- Perpendicular parking
- Reverse parking
- Lane positioning and discipline
- Safe reversing with observation

#### 6. **Scenario-Based Challenges**
- **Night Driving Scenarios** - Limited visibility, headlight usage
- **Wet Weather Scenarios** - Hydroplaning, reduced grip, spray
- **Emergency Braking** - ABS understanding, stopping distance
- **Tire Blow-out** - Vehicle control during failure
- **Loss of Power** - Steering and braking without power assist

#### 7. **Decision-Making Exercises**
- Defensive positioning
- Route planning and hazard avoidance
- Speed management in different scenarios
- Interaction with other road users
- Ethical and legal decision-making

### Simulator Assessment

**Driving Readiness Assessment** measures:
- ✓ Hazard Recognition (ability to spot risks)
- ✓ Speed Management (appropriate speed for conditions)
- ✓ Vehicle Control (smooth, confident handling)
- ✓ Decision-Making (defensive, safe choices)
- ✓ Concentration (sustained attention)
- ✓ Confidence (comfort operating vehicle)

**Pass Criteria:** 70% overall assessment score

## 🚗 PHASE 3: Practical Driving Training

**Status:** Unlocks after Phase 2 completion  
**Duration:** 10+ hours (typically 2-4 weeks)  
**Format:** On-road instruction with licensed instructor  
**Location:** FRSC-accredited driving schools  
**Cost:** Varies by school (typically ₦15,000-₦25,000 for full course)

### Why Practical Training?

Real-world driving instruction bridges the gap between simulator practice and independent driving. Under expert guidance, you'll:
- Apply simulator skills to real traffic
- Develop road awareness and judgment
- Learn to handle unpredictable situations
- Build confidence in actual driving scenarios
- Meet regulatory licensing requirements

### Practical Training Curriculum

#### 1. **Vehicle Handling (2-3 hours)**
Foundation skills in real vehicle:
- Starting and stopping
- Smooth acceleration and deceleration
- Steering control and positioning
- Gear management
- Brake and clutch coordination
- Understanding vehicle dynamics

#### 2. **Urban Driving (3-4 hours)**
City traffic and congestion:
- Navigating busy intersections
- Pedestrian interaction and safety
- Bicyclist and motorcyclist awareness
- Speed management in urban areas
- Parking in limited spaces
- Understanding urban hazards
- Bus lanes and traffic regulations

#### 3. **Highway Driving (2-3 hours)**
Expressway and high-speed navigation:
- Highway entry and merging
- Lane discipline on expressways
- Safe overtaking procedures
- Managing speed over long distances
- Fuel efficiency techniques
- Understanding highway hazards
- Rest and fatigue management

#### 4. **Defensive Driving Practice (2-3 hours)**
Advanced safety techniques:
- Hazard anticipation
- Defensive spacing and positioning
- Weather-related adjustments
- Emergency maneuvers
- Distraction avoidance
- Aggressive driver management
- Fatigue recognition

#### 5. **Parking Techniques (1-2 hours)**
Practical parking skills:
- Parallel parking on real streets
- Perpendicular/angle parking
- Reverse parking in tight spaces
- Bay parking at various angles
- Understanding parking signs and rules
- Reversing with observation
- Parking on slopes

#### 6. **Traffic Interaction (2-3 hours)**
Road user respect and communication:
- Right-of-way understanding
- Hand signals and intentions
- Pedestrian communication
- Emergency vehicle interaction
- Cyclist and motorcyclist courtesy
- Honking and signaling appropriately
- Road rage avoidance

#### 7. **Practical Competency Assessment**
Your instructor will evaluate:
- Vehicle control and smoothness
- Hazard recognition and response
- Speed and distance judgment
- Defensive positioning
- Following traffic rules
- General road safety awareness
- Fitness to drive independently

### The Licensing Process

#### Step 1: Document Submission (Week 1)
- Submit all required documents to FRSC office
- Receive application reference number
- Get confirmation of document receipt

#### Step 2: Application Review (Week 2)
- FRSC reviews your educational records
- Verification of Cubbes training completion
- Cross-reference with driving school records
- Medical clearance verification

#### Step 3: Licensing Interview (Week 3)
- Personal interview with FRSC official
- Verification of identity and details
- Questions about training and learning
- Discussion of driving responsibilities
- Oath-taking for legal obligations

#### Step 4: Theory Test (Optional)
- Written or digital theory examination
- Questions on Nigerian road laws
- Hazard perception scenarios
- Traffic sign identification
- Safe driving principles

#### Step 5: License Issuance (Week 4)
- Biometric capture and identity verification
- License production and printing
- Database registration
- Official license presentation
- Driving privileges confirmation

### FRSC Licensing Classes

Nigeria's driver's licenses are categorized by vehicle type:

- **Private Drive (PVT)** - Cars and light vehicles (up to 3,500 kg)
- **Heavy Truck (HT)** - Heavy commercial vehicles
- **Public Transportation (PT)** - Buses and minibuses
- **Motorcycle (MCL)** - Two and three-wheel vehicles

Your Cubbes course prepares you for **Private Drive (PVT)** class.

### License Validity

- **New License:** Valid for 5 years
- **Renewal:** Valid for another 5 years
- **Medical Renewal:** After 10 years, periodic medical checks required
- **Replacement:** If lost or damaged, FRSC can issue replacement

### Your Rights as a Licensed Driver

✓ Legally permitted to operate a private vehicle  
✓ Right to drive on all public roads  
✓ Compliance with all traffic laws  
✓ Access to formal driving partnerships  
✓ Insurance eligibility  
✓ Employment as a driver if qualified  

### Your Responsibilities as a Licensed Driver

✓ Follow all traffic laws and regulations  
✓ Maintain vehicle in safe condition  
✓ Report accidents to FRSC  
✓ Renew license before expiration  
✓ Carry valid license while driving  
✓ Yield to emergency vehicles  
✓ Respect other road users  
✓ Not drive under influence of alcohol/drugs
